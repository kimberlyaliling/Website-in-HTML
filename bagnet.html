Bagnet

Bagnet is a deep fried crispy pork belly dish that is similar to lechon kawali. It originated from Ilocos and is considered to be a top favorite among Filipinos. This is best served bagoong monamon. it is a dip made of fermented anchovies

Ingrdients

  • 2 Liters of Water.
  • 1 Red Onion, sliced.
  • 1 Small Carrots, sliced.
  • 5-7 Pieces Laurel Leaves.
  • 1 Tbsp of Whole Peppercorn.
  • 2 Tbsp of Himalayan or Sea Salt.
  • 1kg of Pork Belly.

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1. Simmer on medium heat.
  • 2. Once it starts simmering, add the pork belly. Simmer for 40 minutes or until the pork is tender.
  • 3. Remove from the pot and let sit on a cooling rack for a while so the liquid will drain.
  • 4. Prick the skin many times using a fork then dry with paper towels.
  • 5. Using a wok or large frying pan, heat-oil over medium-high heat.
  • 6. Cover right away as the hot oil pops and splatters when combines with liquid. Since the pork is not totally dry, the oil pop when the moisture in cooking oil evaporates fast into gas when hot.

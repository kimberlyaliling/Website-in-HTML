Poqui-Poqui

Poqui Poqui is a traditional Ilocano dish that originated in the Ilocos Region. It is made from grilled eggplant, eggs, tomatoes, and other simple ingredients. The dish is believed to have been influenced by Ilocano workers who migrated to Hawaii, although the exact origin of its name is uncertain.

Ingredient

  • Eggplant
  • Eggs
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Onions
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt
  • Ground Pepper

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1. Grill the eggplants until the skin is charred and the inside is tender.
  • 2. Let the eggplants cool, peel off the skin, and mash the flesh.
  • 3. Sauté the garlic, onions, and tomatoes in cooking oil.
  • 4. Add the mashed eggplant and season with salt and pepper.
  • 5. Beat the eggs and pour them into the pan.
  • 6. Mix and cook until the eggs are fully cooked.

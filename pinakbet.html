Pinakbet

Pinakbet Ilocano is a traditional Filipino vegetable stew from the northern Ilocos region, characterized by its savory, salty, and slightly bitter flavor profile achieved by simmering local vegetables with fermented fish sauce (bagoong isda).

Ingredients

  • 1/4 Pork Belly.
  • 1/4 Kilo Ripe Tomatoes.
  • 1tsp of Salt.
  • 2 1/2 tbsp bagoong isda.
  • 1 clove garlic.
  • 1 small red onion.
  • 5-6 pieces talong bilog (native).
  • 6-8 pieces ampalaya bilog (native).
  • 5-7 pieces okra.
  • 3-4 pieces kamoteng dilaw.
  • 6-8 pieces sigarilyas.
  • 12- 15 pieces siling duwag (native).
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil.

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1. Chop the garlic into small pieces.
  • 2. Slice the onion into thin pieces.
  • 3. Cut the stem of the talong (eggplant) and slice it into pieces.
  • 4. Cut the ampalaya into halves.
  • 5. Trim both ends of the sigarilyas (winged beans).
  • 6. Open the sigarilyas vertically to prepare them for slicing.
  • 7. Cut the sigarilyas in half.
  • 8. Slice the top part of the okra.
  • 9. Peel the camote.
  • 10. Cut the camote into quarters.
  • 11. Put it in water, then set aside.
  • 12. Slice the tomatoes into quarters.
  • 13. Put the sliced tomatoes in a bowl.
  • 14. Add salt.
  • 15. Mash the tomatoes using your hands.
  • 16. Slice the pork belly about 1 inch in size.
  • 17. Put the sliced pork in a casserole.
  • 18. Add water.
  • 19. Set the casserole on low to medium flame.
  • 20. Cover and let this simmer for 10 minutes or until the water is reduced and the pork fat is rendered.
  • 21. Give it a mix.
  • 22. Add oil.
  • 23. Put it on the side.
  • 24. Sauté the chopped garlic.
  • 25. Add the sliced onions.
  • 26. Add the mashed tomatoes.
  • 27. Mix everything.
  • 28. Add the sliced camote.
  • 29. Add the talong.
  • 30. Cover and simmer this for 5 minutes.
  • 31. Add the ampalaya.
  • 32. Add bagoong isda.
  • 33. Cover and cook for 1 minute.
  • 34. Add the sigarilyas.
  • 35. Add the okra.
  • 36. Add the siling duwag (native).
  • 37. Cover and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes.
  • 38. Shake the casserole. It’s the traditional way Ilokanos cook pakbet.
  • 39. Our Pakbet Ilokano is ready to serve. Enjoy!

Dinengdeng

Dinengdeng or inabraw is a Filipino vegetable dish that originated from the Ilocos region in the Philippines. It is composed of different vegetables that are usually grown in one’s backyard. This dish has a similarity with bulanglang and laswa; the obvious difference of Dinengdeng is the use of fried or grilled fish. Another notable difference is the addition of “bagoong isda”.

Ingredients

  • Fried Galunggong.
  • Kalabasa/Squash.
  • Moringa/Malunggay Leaves.
  • Ampalaya Leaves(optional depends on your preference).
  • Bagoong Isda.
  • Sitaw/String Beans
  • Salt

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1. In a casserole, boil two cups of water.
  • 2. Add the fish bagoong, stir, and cover for 1 minute.
  • 3. After 1 minute, add the squash.
  • 4. Cover and cook for 3–5 minutes, depending on how quickly the squash cooks.
  • 5. After 3 minutes, check if the squash is already cooked.
  • 6. Add the string beans and boil for 30 seconds.
  • 7. After 30 seconds, add the two fried galunggong.
  • 8. Add the okra and stir gently.
  • 9. Taste the soup to check if the flavor is already right.
  • 10. If it tastes a little bland, add a small amount of salt according to your taste.
  • 11. Cover and cook for another 30 seconds.
  • 12. After 30 seconds, add the malunggay leaves and bitter melon.
  • 13. Cover for a few more seconds. The dinengdeng is now ready to serve.
